  • 311 has tagged reports as furniture or mattress only since June 13, 2024. Reports count calls, not items: busy, walkable areas with heavy 311 app use report more. Counts are not adjusted for population.
  • "Gone on arrival" is the share of reports Recology closed as unable to locate. Someone may have taken the item, but the location may also have been vague.
  • Shops are open registered businesses with a used-merchandise industry code or a resale-sounding name, with false matches removed by hand. Industry codes are self-reported.
  • Street vending permits are a snapshot of currently active permits only (the program issues roughly 150–180 a year), and unpermitted vending isn't included. There is no DataSF data on garage sales or flea markets.
