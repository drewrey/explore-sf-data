  • Outcomes come from Recology's free-text status notes. "Bulky item pickup" means residents set items out for a scheduled pickup (not dumping); "Building's responsibility" means items were traced to a building that must book its own pickup; "Gone on arrival" means the crew found nothing.
  • Encampment-related reports (notes about homeless people, encampments, tents or people sleeping, or reports handled by the Healthy Streets Operation Center) are hidden by default: they're about people's belongings, not dumped furniture.
  • Reports count calls, not items. Counts aren't adjusted for population, and areas with heavy 311 app use report more.
  • Time to close runs from the report to when the case was closed. Report locations are as submitted.
